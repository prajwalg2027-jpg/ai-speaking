Privacy Policy

Adam, the English speaking coach · Last updated {{LAST_UPDATED}}

This policy explains what we collect when you use the Adam app (“Adam”, “we”), why, and your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in with a one-time code (sent by Google Firebase).
  • Your voice recordings while you answer Adam, and the text of what you said.
  • Your results: your English level and the scores for each answer.
  • What you tell Adam about yourself in conversation (for example your name, city or work), and details you give us in the app.
  • Sakhi’s notes about you: when you practise, Sakhi (Adam’s tutor) keeps short written notes about what you share, such as your work, your goals, plans you mention, and what you like, plus a one-line summary of each conversation. These are text only, never audio. She never keeps notes about your health, religion, caste, politics, money, relationships or contact details.
  • How you use the app: for example when you open it, start a test or see your result.

Adam uses the microphone only while you are answering. This website (adamspeaks.in) uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Why

  • To run the conversation, work out your English level, and show you what to practise.
  • To make practice personal: Sakhi uses her notes to remember you between conversations (for example, to ask how your interview went).
  • To check and improve how well Adam understands and scores learners.
  • To keep the service safe and working, and to prevent misuse.

Who processes it

Your voice and the text of your answers are sent to Sarvam AI to turn speech into text, to generate Adam’s voice, and to understand and score your answers. Your account and data are stored on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase. We don’t sell your data.

How long we keep it

Sakhi’s notes are kept until you delete them or your account. Conversation summaries are deleted after 12 months.

We keep your account, results and voice recordings while your account exists, so we can follow your progress and improve Adam. When you delete your account, we delete your recordings, your personal details and the text of your answers. We keep only anonymous scores that can’t be linked to you.

Your rights

  • See what we hold about you, and correct it.
  • See and delete Sakhi’s notes about you at any time: in the app, open What Sakhi remembers about you. You can delete one note or all of them, or switch her memory off. You can also tell Sakhi “forget that” while you talk.
  • Delete your account and data at any time: in the app, tap the account button, then Delete my account and data, or write to us. See how to delete your account.
  • Withdraw your consent (this means you can no longer use Adam).
  • Raise a complaint with us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Age

Adam is for people aged 18 or older.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app about important changes.
