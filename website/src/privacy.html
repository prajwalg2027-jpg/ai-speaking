3. Microphone access

Your browser asks for permission before AdamSpeaks can use your microphone. Audio is captured only after you tap to start talking, and only while a session is active. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your browser or phone settings. Without it, the speaking coach can't work, but the rest of the website stays available.

4. Who processes your data for us

We use a small number of service providers ("Data Processors") who act only on our instructions:

  • Sarvam AI (India) handles speech-to-text, translation, text-to-speech and the language models that understand and score your answers. Your audio and transcripts are sent to Sarvam AI over an encrypted connection for this purpose only. Their own handling is described in Sarvam AI's privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud / Firebase hosts this website and the Service and keeps standard server logs.
  • Our email provider receives and stores the emails you send us.

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it with others only when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid legal order.

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside India. Where that happens, we rely on transfers that are permitted under the DPDP Act and on the provider's contractual safeguards.

5. AI and your recordings

AdamSpeaks uses AI to listen, understand and respond. We do not use your recordings or transcripts to train AI models, and we do not keep them to build a profile of you. If we ever want to use samples to improve the Service, we'll ask for your separate, explicit consent first, and saying no won't affect your access.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts. All fonts, images and code are served from our own domain. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy and ask for consent where it's required.

7. Children

AdamSpeaks is intended for people aged 18 and over. Younger learners may use it only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, as required by the DPDP Act. If you believe a child has used the Service without that consent, contact us and we'll delete any related data we hold.

8. How we protect your data

  • All traffic to adamspeaks.in and to our providers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Our access keys for AI services stay on our servers and are never sent to your browser.
  • We keep as little personal data as possible. Session data lives only in memory and is discarded at the end of the session.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we'll notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you can:

  • Access: ask what personal data we hold about you and how we've used it.
  • Correct or update data that's inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that already happened.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you're not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Because we don't keep session recordings or transcripts, there is usually nothing from your speaking sessions to retrieve or delete. We'll confirm this, and act on any other data we do hold, such as your emails. We'll reply within 30 days.
