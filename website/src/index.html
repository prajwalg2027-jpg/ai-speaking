How it works

Find your speaking level in about four minutes.

Your first conversation is a short, friendly chat. There's no written test and no red marks. Each question adapts to how you answered the last one, so it never feels too easy or too hard.

  1. Say hello

    Tell the coach about yourself, in English or Hindi. How you choose to answer is the first clue to your level.

    Tell me your name, and what you do during the day.

  2. Name a few words

    A picture or a word appears on screen. Say it in English, or explain what it means. This shows what you know beyond full sentences.

    'Paani' ko English mein kya kehte hain?

  3. Read one sentence

    Read a short sentence out loud. We check how clearly it comes across, not whether you have a "foreign" accent.

    Every morning I take the bus to work.

  4. Share an opinion

    Talk freely about something you care about. Unplanned speech shows how far your English can really go.

    If you could change one thing about your city, what would it be?

Four levels

Start where you are. Grow from there.

After the chat you'll get one of four levels and a short note on what to work on next. You won't see a score out of 100.

LEVEL 1

Starter

Little or no English yet. Instructions come in Hindi, and you build your first words and phrases with lots of support.

LEVEL 2

Word-builder

You know plenty of words but sentences come slowly. Practise joining them up, with fewer pauses and the right tenses.

LEVEL 3

Conversational

You hold a conversation comfortably with a few slips. Work on grammar polish and a richer vocabulary.

LEVEL 4

Eloquent

Precise, varied and idiomatic. Sharpen how you argue, persuade and present, for interviews, meetings and talks.

Built for Indian learners

A patient coach that speaks your language too.

Most English apps were built for someone else. AdamSpeaks runs on speech technology trained on Indian voices, and it's designed to keep you talking.

Hindi when you're stuck

Didn't catch a question? Say so in English or Hindi. The coach repeats it slowly, then explains it in Hindi. You can mix the two as you go.

Your accent is welcome

We measure whether you're understood, not whether you sound British or American. Clear Indian English is good English.

No red marks

While you're finding your level there's no "right" or "wrong", just warm replies like "Nice!" and "Got it". Mistakes are where learning starts.

Never left in silence

If you go quiet, the coach gives you a gentle nudge, offers the question in Hindi, and always moves on kindly. You finish every session on a win.
