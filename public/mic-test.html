Mic test: phone audio processing on vs off

STT lab · App

  1. Pick where you are.
  2. Each sentence has two record buttons: processing ON (the phone's noise suppression and auto volume) and processing OFF. Tap one, read the sentence at your normal distance from the phone, then tap Stop. Do the same with the other button.
  3. When you're done, tap Copy results at the bottom and paste them to Claude.

Echo cancellation stays on in both, as in the app. The order alternates so practice doesn't favour one side. Accuracy is what matters: how much of the sentence Sarvam heard correctly.
