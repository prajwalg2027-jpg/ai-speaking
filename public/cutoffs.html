Level cut-offs

These numbers decide which level a learner is placed at. Change any of them, then download the file and give it to Claude. Nothing here changes the app until the file is put in.

Every skill is scored from 1 (weak) to 4 (strong). The skills are averaged into one overall score, which is shown in the team view's log at the end of each conversation as “adjusted score”.
